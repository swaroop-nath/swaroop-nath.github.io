About

Hi! I am a Member of Technical Staff at Reflection AI, working on reasoning post-training.

Before this, I spent two years at Google DeepMind, in the Machine Learning and Optimization team led by Dr. Prateek Jain, where I worked on Efficiency -- both in Pre-training and Post-training. My most significant work was on making Gemini's reasoning process efficient (more details in CV).

I graduated as an MS by Research scholar from the CFILT Lab, IIT Bombay. I was fortunate enough to be advised by Dr. Pushpak Bhattacharyya (God rest his soul) and Dr. Harshad Khadilkar. My Thesis was recognized with the Winifred B. Fernandes Research Excellence Award. My works have been featured in A* NLP conferences — EMNLP and ACL.
