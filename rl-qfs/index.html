Abstract

Query-focused Summarization (QfS) deals with systems that generate summaries from document(s) based on a query. Motivated by the insight that Reinforcement Learning (RL) provides a generalization to Supervised Learning (SL) for Natural Language Generation, and thereby performs better (empirically) than SL, we use an RL-based approach for this task of QfS. Additionally, we also resolve the conflict of employing RL in Transformers with Teacher Forcing. We develop multiple Policy Gradient networks, trained on various reward signals: ROUGE, BLEU, and Semantic Similarity, which lead to a 10-point improvement over the State-of-the-Art approach on the ROUGE-L metric for a benchmark dataset (ELI5). We also show performance of our approach in zero-shot setting for another benchmark dataset (DebatePedia) – our approach leads to results comparable to baselines, which were specifically trained on DebatePedia. To aid the RL training, we propose a better semantic similarity reward, enabled by a novel Passage Embedding scheme developed using Cluster Hypothesis. Lastly, we contribute a gold-standard test dataset to further research in QfS and Long-form Question Answering (LfQA).

SfPEG: Similarity From Passage EmbeddinG

Averaging sentence embeddings gives only an approximate passage representation. SfPEG instead trains a passage embedder on the Cluster Hypothesis — passages that answer the same information need should cluster together. A BERT-large dual encoder learns to tell whether two Reddit answers reply to the same question; the reward is the cosine similarity between the embeddings of the generated and reference summaries.

Does the Model Read the Document?

On RQFT, replacing the document with an unrelated one costs the supervised baseline 5.4 ROUGE-L points but the RL models 15.8–17.8. The paper reads this as the RL models summarising the document, while the supervised model largely answers from the query alone (Section 6.3).

In human evaluation of 50 RQFT samples by two annotators, BART R-SfPEG scored highest: fluency 3.46 / 3.48 out of 4 and correctness 0.48 / 0.44, against 3.08 / 3.12 and 0.26 / 0.28 for the supervised BART SL.

Datasets

RQFT · Reliable QfS Tester

250 hand-curated query–document–summary triplets from Wikipedia and high-school textbooks, across 13 domains. Documents carry 1.41 queries on average, to avoid topic centralisation.

RPEDT · Reddit Passage Embedding DataseT

Questions and their answers scraped from 39 subreddits (154,722 training questions), used to train the SfPEG passage embedder.
