Research & Industry Talks

  • 2024

    Task-Aligned Reward Modeling for Reinforcement Learning in Text Summarization

    Industry talk · Hyperbots

    Supervised training rewards a summary only for matching one reference, even though the same content can be written many ways. Reinforcement learning lets us optimise any reward instead, which raises the real question: which reward? This talk argues that the properties of the task itself should define the rewards, and shows it for two settings — query-focused summarization and opinion summarization.

  • January 2024

    Leveraging Domain Knowledge for Efficient Reward Modelling in RLHF

    RISC 2024, Dept. of CSE, IIT Bombay Best Talk Award

    Reward models for RLHF usually need large amounts of human-preference data. Domain experts can instead name the features that make an output good, and those features compress much of the variation in the data. For e-commerce opinion summarization, seven such features let us train the reward model on just 940 preference pairs — a more than 20× reduction — while improving summary quality.

  • 2024

    Cut to the Chase, Please: Summarizing Textual Data

    Lecture to IIT Bombay students, with the Summarization Team at CFILT Lab

    A tour of text summarization: why it matters as digital text keeps growing, and its two goals of coverage and conciseness. It moves from statistical and neural extractive methods to abstractive sequence-to-sequence models, attention, PEGASUS pre-training and pointer-generator networks, then covers evaluation with ROUGE and reinforcement learning for query-focused and opinion summarization.

  • 2024

    Reinforcement Learning in Natural Language Generation

    Lecture to IIT Bombay students

    Why generation needs more than token-by-token matching: what makes text good, and why humans act as the oracle. It covers preference modelling and the Bradley–Terry model, the InstructGPT recipe of supervised fine-tuning followed by RLHF, and how domain knowledge can cut down the preference data a reward model needs.

    Alignment, Preference Modelling, RLHF

    Why maximum likelihood teaches only an input-to-output mapping, and what it takes to capture the implicit intent behind a request. It goes from instruction tuning to preference modelling and the REINFORCE trick, walks through InstructGPT and Sparrow, and closes with newer directions such as DPO and ReST.

  • August 2023

    Tips and Tricks for LLM Training

    Practical lessons from pre-training a language model on about 850,000 sentences: larger batches help, learning-rate warmup works best at roughly a fifth of training, and cleaning the data reduces gradient noise. It pairs these with findings from scaling-law research on how model size, data and compute trade off.
